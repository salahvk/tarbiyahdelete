Delete Your Account - Tarbiyah Kerala

This page explains how to delete your Tarbiyah Kerala account and what happens to your data after deletion.

How to Delete Your Account

Step 1 (Recommended): Open the Tarbiyah Kerala app and go to the account settings/profile section.
Step 2: Tap the Delete Account option and confirm your request.
In-app deletion is the primary method. Once confirmed, your account deletion process starts immediately.

If You Cannot Access the App

Alternative: Send an email to mskvk9@gmail.com with the details below:
  • Subject line: "Account Deletion Request"
  • Your email address associated with your Tarbiyah Kerala account
  • Your name (as shown in the app)
  • Confirmation that you want to permanently delete your account
Processing Time: Email-based requests are processed within 30 days. We will send confirmation once deletion is completed.

Contact Us

Email us at: mskvk9@gmail.com

What Data Will Be Deleted?

When your account is deleted, the following data is permanently removed:

  • Personal Information: Email address, display name, profile image, and user ID
  • Child Profiles: Linked child profile data managed in your account
  • Associated Records: Related profile records, images, and entries connected to your account

What Data May Be Retained Temporarily?

Note: Limited data may be retained temporarily for legal or security requirements:

  • Legal Compliance: Data required by applicable law may be retained for the required duration.
  • Security/Fraud Prevention: Minimal records may be kept for abuse prevention and platform security.

Data Retention Period

After account deletion is requested:

  • Your account and associated personal data are deleted, generally within 30 days
  • Legally required data may be retained only as necessary

Alternative: Partial Data Deletion

If you don't want to delete your entire account but want to remove specific data, please contact us at mskvk9@gmail.com and specify what data you'd like removed. We'll do our best to accommodate your request.
